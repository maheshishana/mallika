THE PERSON BEHIND THE STORIES

Curious about the world.
Committed to asking why.

I’m Mahesh Ishana, an independent content creator building documentaries and articles around the questions that shape everyday life.

Why this website exists

We live surrounded by information, strong opinions and quick conclusions. But the most useful questions often need more than a headline or a viral clip. This website is a home for my creative work: documentary ideas, explainers and reflections on society, money, relationships and digital life.

What I want to create

My goal is to make complex topics easier to understand without treating people as statistics or reducing a complicated issue to a simple villain. I want each piece to leave readers with clearer context, useful questions and a reason to think for themselves.

  • Documentary storytelling: turning a focused question into a clear narrative.
  • Financial awareness: explaining everyday money decisions in accessible language.
  • Digital society: examining how online platforms shape attention, trust and behaviour.
  • Practical learning: sharing ideas that can help people make more informed decisions.

My approach

I aim to distinguish facts from interpretation, avoid sensational claims that evidence cannot support, and update content when meaningful corrections are needed. Not every topic has one easy answer, and responsible storytelling should make that clear.

A work in progress

This is an independent journey. The site and its work will grow over time as new research, videos and articles are published. I value thoughtful feedback, relevant sources and constructive collaboration.
