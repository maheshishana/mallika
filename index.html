01THE PERSON BEHIND THE CAMERA

I believe every
big story starts
with a question.

The world is full of stories we scroll past, accept at face value or rarely stop to question.

Through documentary-style videos and articles, I explore these stories with curiosity, clarity and a human perspective. From money and social pressure to relationships and digital life, the aim is to make complicated subjects easier to understand.

This is an independent creative journey: learning, researching, filming and getting better with every story.
