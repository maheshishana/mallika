Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, how this website is designed to handle visitors’ information.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to maheshishana.in (“the website”), operated under the personal brand Mahesh Ishana. It is a general template for this static website and should be reviewed if new tools, forms, analytics, advertising or other services are added.

Information you provide

The contact form on this version of the website prepares a message in your device’s default email application. It does not submit form contents to a website server. If you send an email or contact a linked social account, the information you choose to share will be handled by the relevant email or platform provider.

Automatically collected information

The website itself is made of static files. The hosting provider or infrastructure services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security logs as part of serving and protecting the website. Their own policies and settings govern that processing.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally set first-party analytics or advertising cookies in the supplied version. External services, embedded content, or hosting infrastructure may use their own technologies if introduced or enabled. This policy must be updated if analytics, advertising tags, embedded videos or other third-party tools are added.

External links

Links to platforms such as YouTube and Instagram take you to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not by this website. Review their policies before sharing information.

Children’s privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information through a channel associated with this site, contact the site operator so the concern can be reviewed.

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to submit information or follow external links. For privacy-related questions, use the contact options on the Contact page. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information in a general enquiry.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when the website or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date should be revised whenever material changes are made.

Important: This is a practical starting template, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance with every law that may apply to your specific website or business.
